About

Built around bikes, service, and good local energy

Yowie Bicycle Workshop is a neighbourhood-style bike workshop focused on practical mechanical support and friendly service. The goal is simple: keep riders moving with honest advice and quality work.

What matters here

Clear communication. Solid workmanship. No upsell theatre. Whether you ride daily, hit the trails on weekends, or just want the family bikes working properly again, the workshop is about getting the job done right.
