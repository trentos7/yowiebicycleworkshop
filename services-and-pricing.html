Important Notes on Parts & Servicing

  • Parts Excluded: All listed prices cover labor only. Required replacement parts (e.g., chains, cassettes, brake pads, cables, discs) will be replaced as part of service. Major components always quoted and approved before installation.
  • E-Bikes & Dual Suspension: Add $30 surcharge on Standard Services to cover additional linkage torque checks and e-system motor/drivetrain checks.
  • After-Hours Drop-Off: Flexible, secure contact-free drop-off and collection options available upon request when booking.
