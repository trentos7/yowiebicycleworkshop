Why it matters

Dirty oil and worn seals cost you grip and money

Fork and shock seals keep dirt and water out while the oil inside keeps everything sliding smoothly. Over time the seals wear and the oil breaks down. Your suspension starts to feel harsh, loses air or develops stiction, and grit can score the stanchions. Replacing stanchions costs far more than a regular service.

We recommend a lower leg and air can service about every 50 hours of riding. Ride a few times a week and those hours add up quickly.

Signs your suspension needs a service

  • Feels harsh or sticky over small bumps
  • Oil or grime ring on the stanchions
  • Fork or shock loses air between rides
  • Squelching or knocking noises
  • No service in the last 50 hours of riding

Suspension FAQs

Common questions

How much does a fork service cost?

A Fox or RockShox Fork Lower Leg Service is $160, including the wiper seal kit. A Rear Shock Air Can Service is $140, including the air sleeve seal kit.

How often should I service my suspension?

About every 50 hours of riding for a lower leg or air can service. Service it sooner if it feels harsh, loses air or leaves oil on the stanchions.

Do you do full damper rebuilds?

For damper overhauls and brands other than Fox and RockShox, we recommend Cannon Shock Works and can arrange it as part of a bike service.
