Why e-bikes need more care

More weight, more speed, more torque

E-bikes are heavier than regular bikes, travel at higher average speeds and put far more torque through the chain, cassette and brakes. Wear items go faster, and brakes, wheels and bolts work harder. Regular servicing keeps your e-bike safe, smooth and reliable.

  • Wheels built for the load: spoke tension checks and truing for rear hub motors and heavy payloads
  • Tyres and tubes: e-bike specific tyres and tubes in stock

What we don't do

We take care of all mechanical repairs, adjustments and wear-and-tear parts. We don't repair motor internals, and we don't rebuild batteries or electronics. For those, contact your bike's brand or dealer.

E-Bike FAQs

Common questions

How much does an e-bike service cost?

Our Standard Workshop Service is $150 + parts, plus $30 for e-bikes, so $180 + parts. A Basic Tune-Up & Safety Check is $110, and the Ultimate Service & Custom Rebuild is $400 + parts.

Can you fix my e-bike's motor or battery?

No. We service everything mechanical: drivetrain, brakes, wheels, tyres, bearings and suspension. We don't open motor housings or rebuild batteries or electronics.

Can you pick up my e-bike?

Yes. Our van collects and returns bikes across the Northern Beaches, from $20 each way in the local zone. Pick-up is included with the Ultimate Service in the local zone.

How do I book?

Text or call 0485 022 101 with your e-bike's make and model and when you'd like it done. Drop-off in North Narrabeen is by appointment.
